Questions people ask first.

Why are units sold at a discount?Money in an AIF is locked in for years. Sellers who need liquidity early accept a lower price than NAV.
Does SXchange hold my money?No. You agree terms with the other side directly, and the fund manager records the transfer.
Who can see my details?Only the people you choose. Buyers see your listing, not your name, until you share your details.
Can I sell only part of my units?Yes. List any number of units and set a minimum lot, so buyers can take a slice.
